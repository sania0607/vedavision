# Yoga Pose Reference Guide

## 🧘 Supported Poses

### 1. Tree Pose (Vrksasana) 🌳
**Key Points:**
- Stand on one leg
- Place the other foot on inner thigh or calf (avoid the knee)
- Keep standing leg straight
- Balance with hands in prayer position or raised overhead
- Keep hips square and shoulders level

**Common Mistakes:**
- Leaning to one side
- Bending the standing leg
- Placing foot directly on knee joint

---

### 2. Warrior II (Virabhadrasana II) ⚔️
**Key Points:**
- Wide stance with feet parallel
- Front knee bent at 90 degrees (knee over ankle)
- Back leg straight and strong
- Arms extended parallel to floor
- Shoulders squared and level
- Gaze over front hand

**Common Mistakes:**
- Front knee extending past ankle
- Leaning torso forward
- Arms not fully extended
- Shoulders not level

---

### 3. Triangle Pose (Trikonasana) 📐
**Key Points:**
- Wide stance with feet parallel
- Both legs completely straight
- Reach torso to the side (not forward)
- Bottom hand reaches toward ankle/shin/floor
- Top arm extends straight up
- Both sides of torso equally long

**Common Mistakes:**
- Bending the knees
- Collapsing through the torso
- Rotating chest downward instead of opening

---

### 4. Downward Facing Dog (Adho Mukha Svanasana) 🐕
**Key Points:**
- Inverted V-shape
- Hands shoulder-width apart, fingers spread
- Feet hip-width apart
- Hips are the highest point
- Legs straight (or slightly bent if hamstrings are tight)
- Spine long and neutral
- Head relaxed between arms

**Common Mistakes:**
- Rounding the back
- Shoulders too close to ears
- Weight too far forward
- Locked elbows

---

### 5. Mountain Pose (Tadasana) ⛰️
**Key Points:**
- Feet together or hip-width apart
- Weight evenly distributed
- Legs straight and strong
- Shoulders back and down
- Arms at sides or overhead
- Body in one vertical line
- Chin parallel to floor

**Common Mistakes:**
- Leaning forward or back
- Locking knees too hard
- Tension in shoulders
- Uneven weight distribution

---

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

1. **Good Lighting**: Ensure your practice area is well-lit
2. **Camera Position**: Position camera 6-8 feet away, at chest height
3. **Full Body Visible**: Make sure your entire body is in frame
4. **Solid Background**: Practice against a plain background for better detection
5. **Fitted Clothing**: Wear form-fitting clothes for better pose detection
6. **Steady Camera**: Use a tripod or stable surface for your camera

## 🎯 Understanding the Feedback

- **Score 80-100**: Excellent form! Keep holding the pose
- **Score 60-79**: Good attempt, minor adjustments needed
- **Score below 60**: Significant corrections needed for safety and effectiveness

## ⚠️ Safety Tips

- Always warm up before practicing
- Listen to your body - never force a pose
- Modify poses as needed for your body
- If you experience pain (not discomfort), stop immediately
- Consult with a yoga instructor or healthcare provider if you have any concerns
